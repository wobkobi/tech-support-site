TO THE POINT TECH - LEFT CHEST EMBROIDERY
=========================================

WHAT IS ON THIS DRIVE
  1 - Vector artwork (digitise from these)   Every option as both SVG and PDF, drawn at finished size
                                              in millimetres. The two match, so use whichever your
                                              software prefers. The navy in the PDFs is the garment
                                              colour, shown so the white thread is visible. It is not
                                              a shape to be stitched.
  2 - Previews on navy                        What each option looks like stitched on a navy garment.
  3 - PNG (transparent, 600dpi)               Raster back-up only. Use the vectors if you can.

  Each option has a code (A1, B3 and so on) at the start of its filename, the same in all three
  folders and on the proof page. The finished size is at the end of every filename. C1 is left out
  on purpose: it is too fine to stitch.
  These are artwork files, not stitch files. Please digitise to DST/PES, and send me a photo of a
  test stitch-out on the actual garment before the order runs.

  Stitch counts are my estimates as a sanity check only. Your digitised file is the real number.
  A3, A4, B2 and B3 need the height as well as the width, so please check the frame takes both.
  B2 and B3 are 100 mm deep, which is more than a left chest usually carries. They may suit a centre
  front or back better.

GARMENTS
  AS Colour 5411 cotton pique polo (240 gsm), navy.
  AS Colour 5125 half-zip fleece (350 gsm cotton / recycled polyester), navy.
  Placement: left chest. On the 5125, keep the nearest edge of the logo about 25 mm clear of the zip.

THREAD
  2 colours, polyester (holds the orange through warm washes better than rayon).
    White: the chip, and TECH SUPPORT on A3, A4, B2, B3 and C2.
    Orange, matched as closely as you can to hex #F34213: the rest of the lettering and the divider.
  Orange varies a lot between thread brands, so please show me the spool before running.
  The SVG layers are named thread-white then thread-orange, in stitch order.

STITCH
  Satin throughout: letters, chip lines, pins and divider. No fills needed.
  The chip pins match the logo: 12 per side in three groups of four (A1 has 6, as its chip
  is too small for 12). Please keep the slightly wider gaps between the groups.
  The gap between the chip's two frames equals the frame line, the same as the gap between pins.
  Every line has been checked at finished size: thinnest line 1.2 mm or more, narrowest gap
  1.3 mm or more, widest satin column 3.6 mm, so nothing needs splitting.
  The tightest spots are the chamfered letter corners and the diagonal of the N in POINT.
  Rounding those off as normal is fine.

BACKING AND TOPPING
  Cut-away backing on both garments (tear-away lets the logo pucker after a few washes).
  Water-soluble topping on both, so the stitches sit on the pique and the fleece nap.

SIZING
  Each file is already at the finished size. If a size has to change, scale the whole design as one
  group, and check with me first: the line weights are set close to the minimum a satin will hold,
  so shrinking any option takes its lines under that.
